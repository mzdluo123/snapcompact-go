model billing and image renderer. omp/catalog.json snapshots the 18.22.2
bundled vision-model configuration and API defaults, deduplicated by policy.

crates/pi-natives/src/fonts directory. See omp/UPSTREAM-LICENSE and
fonts/Silver.LICENSE. Silver outlines are rasterized with a Go translation of
Fontdue 0.9.4 geometry and raster math. See omp/FONTDUE-LICENSE.

recorded from the official package, with SHA-256 of decoded RGB pixels.
PNG compression and session-level message selection belong to the encoder
and caller, respectively.
