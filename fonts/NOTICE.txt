Font layout here uses Go's OpenType rasterizer and grapheme segmentation, not
HarfBuzz shaping. Chinese/ASCII and scalar glyph coverage are supported;
complex-script shaping, emoji sequences, bidirectional layout, and precise
combining-mark anchor positioning are not claimed. Missing scalar glyphs cause
an explicit error with source IDs and codepoints rather than silent substitution.
